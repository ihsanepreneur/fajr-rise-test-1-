Honest limitation: reminders (chime, voice, or notification) can only fire while this app is open, minimized, or very recently backgrounded on your phone. Phones aggressively stop background browser tabs to save battery, and iOS Safari is stricter about this than Android. There is no free way to guarantee an alert wakes your phone hours later while the app is fully closed — that reliably needs either push notifications from a paid server, or a native app with OS-level alarm access. For now, the most dependable habit is keeping the app open (screen can be off) overnight, or using your phone's own alarm as a backup alongside this.
